# Research and Citation Policy

## Purpose

Define how library authors establish and communicate platform truth.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Salesforce official documentation.
2. Salesforce release notes.
3. Salesforce developer documentation.
4. Verified platform behavior with reproducible context.
5. Community consensus, clearly labelled as non-authoritative.

## Rules

- Cite an official source for every capability, limit, API behavior, and context-specific claim that may change.
- Include a direct link in an **Official References** section.
- Mark unverified behavior as uncertain; do not convert inference into fact.
- Record the applicable product, execution context, Business Unit assumptions, and verification date for observed behavior.
- Do not use a search result, forum answer, or code sample as sole proof of capability.
- Recheck time-sensitive claims during review, especially limits, entitlement, release, API, and channel behavior.

## Review standard

Reviewers verify that recommendations conform to [Platform facts](../SFMC_PLATFORM_FACTS.md), that examples are production-oriented, and that citations directly support the claims made.
